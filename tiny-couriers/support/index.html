Frequently asked

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted too.

How do I turn off music or sounds, or change the language?

Tap the gear button at the top right. Music, sound effects, music volume and language are all there.

How does the manager work?

Hire a manager in the Staff panel. While the game is closed, the manager keeps delivering orders and you collect the earnings when you come back.

Can I stop the game to plan?

Yes. The pause button next to the gear freezes the map: you can still open menus, collect coins and assign orders. Press play to continue.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads are optional; they only appear when you choose to double your offline earnings.
