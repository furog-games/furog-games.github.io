Privacy Policy — Tiny Couriers

Effective date: November 16, 2026 · Developer: Furog Games

This policy explains what information the game Tiny Couriers ("the app") handles. In short: the game itself does not collect personal data and has no accounts. It shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to serve those ads.

1. Data the game stores

Your progress (money, couriers, upgrades, settings such as music volume and language) is saved only on your device. We do not run servers, we do not have user accounts, and this data is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app can show an optional rewarded video ad (for example to double your offline earnings). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers — the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in the iOS "Allow app to track" prompt;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device information (model, operating system, language) and interactions with ads;
  • diagnostic data such as crashes and performance.

If you do not allow tracking, ads are still shown but are not personalized with the IDFA. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Google's use of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

4. Your rights

Because we do not hold any of your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. For data processed by Google for advertising, use the controls described in Google's policies, or reset your advertising identifier in iOS settings.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
