Marble Mosaic — Support

Found a bug, have an idea, or need help? Write to us at marinariogroup@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the level number, and a screenshot if you can. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked

How do I play?

Tap a tray on the board. Its marbles roll through the funnel and drop into the cup of the same color. Clear every tray to complete the picture. If too many marbles are waiting, the funnel overflows and you can try again.

What are mystery and frozen trays?

Grey mystery trays hide their color until the tray in front of them is cleared. Frozen trays can't be tapped until the number on them reaches zero — every move melts the ice a little.

How do Undo and +2 Funnel work?

You get 3 free undos in every level; after that an undo costs coins. +2 Funnel adds two extra spaces to the funnel once per level. If you don't have enough coins, you can watch an optional ad instead.

How do I earn coins?

Finish a level for the first time, and come back every day for the daily reward — it grows over 7 days in a row.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted too.

How do I turn off music, sounds or vibration, or change the language?

Tap the gear button at the top right. Music, sound effects, vibration and language are all there.

Do I have to watch ads?

Rewarded ads are always optional. From level 4, a short ad may appear between levels from time to time.
